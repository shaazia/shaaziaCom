How I approach design

I’m most effective in ambiguous problem spaces—where goals aren’t fully defined and constraints are real. I work closely with product, engineering, and research to shape direction early, then stay hands-on through execution.

  • I start with intent and mental models before jumping to UI
  • I design for trust, especially in AI, finance, and support contexts
  • I prioritize systems and patterns over one-off screens
  • I believe clarity is a feature, not a polish step
