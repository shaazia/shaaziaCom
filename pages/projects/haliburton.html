Haliburton

App and workflow design for industrial solutions.

The Challenge

Halliburton's field operations teams struggled with outdated systems that didn't work in low-connectivity environments. Critical data was difficult to access, manual processes were error-prone, and teams spent more time managing paperwork than solving problems.

Our Approach

We designed a mobile-first application that works offline and syncs when connectivity returns. Our UI emphasizes rapid data entry, decision support, and real-time visibility into operations. The new system reduced paperwork, decreased errors, and enabled field teams to focus on high-value work.
