American Eagle Outfitter

UI and design work for retail experience transformation.

The Challenge

American Eagle needed to modernize their mobile retail experience to better compete with direct-to-consumer brands. The existing interface was outdated and did not support the customer's desire to browse, try, and purchase seamlessly across devices.

Our Approach

We designed a fresh, engaging mobile interface that showcases products with high-quality imagery and clear calls-to-action. The new design emphasizes brand storytelling, simplifies the purchase flow, and integrates social commerce features to drive engagement and increase conversion rates.
