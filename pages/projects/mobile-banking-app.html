Mobile Banking App

UI and workflow design for mobile banking solutions.

The Challenge

Customers were struggling to complete banking transactions on mobile devices due to complex workflows and unclear navigation. The existing interface required too many steps to perform simple tasks like transfers, bill payments, and account inquiries, leading to abandoned transactions and support tickets.

Our Approach

We redesigned the mobile banking experience with a focus on task-based navigation and clear information hierarchy. By streamlining workflows, reducing cognitive load, and providing contextual guidance, we increased task completion rates and reduced customer support inquiries. The new interface emphasizes transparency in financial transactions while maintaining security and compliance.
