My role

Staff-level IC leading conversational and interaction design

I led interaction and conversational design for high-volume initiatives spanning AI chatbots, the Customer Service homepage, and cross-channel digital, device, and Alexa support.

Unknown charges

Reduce ambiguity without dumping a transaction list

Approximately half a million customers contact Customer Service each year to investigate unknown charges. Presenting a long list of transactions increases cognitive load and slows identification.

We designed a guided conversational flow that progressively narrowed context through targeted questions, accelerating time-to-identification and enabling clear next-step actions once the charge was found.

Impact: Reduced unknown-charge contacts by ~30% through automation and clearer self-service paths.

Subscription management

Move high-intent tasks into the app

Customers previously had to use desktop or contact support to cancel, switch plans, or change add-ons. We introduced in-app automation for Prime Video, Amazon Music, Kindle Unlimited, and Kids+.

Signal: Improved task completion in-chat and reduced repeat contacts by making “what happened?” and “what can I do?” feel immediate and obvious.
