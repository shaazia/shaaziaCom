Case Study

Amazon Customer Service (Confidential Work)

A collection of confidential initiatives designing AI-driven customer support across chat, voice, and devices— focused on reducing customer effort, increasing automation, and scaling trusted interaction patterns.

AI Chatbots Voice UX Multimodal Future Vision Devices & Entertainment

Context

This page represents multiple Amazon Customer Service programs that are not yet public. The work spans conversational AI design, voice-first support, and future-vision concepts across customer service surfaces. The shared goal: help customers resolve issues quickly and confidently while encouraging responsible AI automation.

The design goal: make automated support feel clear, trustworthy, and action-oriented—especially in high-stakes moments like billing confusion, subscription changes, and device troubleshooting.

My role

  • Staff-level IC design across chat, voice, and device surfaces
  • Interaction design, content strategy, and workflow definition
  • Prototyping and concept exploration in ambiguous problem spaces
  • Partnership with product, engineering, and research

Project areas

1) AI customer service chatbots (confidential)

Designed AI-driven chatbot experiences for high-frequency customer service needs including subscription management and unknown charges. The focus was on reducing friction, building trust, and translating complex account/system signals into clear “next best actions.”

  • Subscription management: status clarity, plan changes, eligibility explanations, confirmation states
  • Unknown charges: investigation support, guided resolution paths, reassurance + escalation options
  • Key UX themes: explainability, error recovery, confidence-building language, reduced handoffs

2) Future-vision customer service landing page

Explored an AI-first customer service landing experience that proactively surfaces relevant help, adapts based on customer context, and uses intelligent prediction to reduce customer service contacts. Concepts included multimodal interactions, personalization, and proactive guidance to encourage AI automation responsibly.

  • Proactive assistance that reduces “search and guess” behavior
  • Personalized help modules based on context and intent
  • Guided paths that balance automation with transparency and control

3) Customer service designs for Alexa+ devices

Designed voice-first support concepts optimized for Alexa+ surfaces, focusing on conversational clarity, repair strategies for ambiguity, and multimodal handoffs when a task benefits from visuals or deeper account detail.

  • Voice interaction patterns for support tasks (not just Q&A)
  • Graceful failure states and “conversation repair” flows
  • Cross-surface handoffs that keep customers oriented and in control

4) Future-vision support for Fire TV & Echo family devices

Led future-state explorations for customer service across Fire TV and Echo family devices, including concepts for embedded support in entertainment contexts and visions for deeper integration with Prime Video. The goal was to reduce the need to “leave the experience” to get help—especially when attention is high.

  • Context-aware support surfaced at the moment of friction
  • Help embedded into entertainment flows without breaking immersion
  • Patterns for consistent support across device families

Design principles across the work

  • Reduce cognitive load: translate system complexity into human language
  • Design for ambiguity: clear recovery paths and safe defaults
  • Make AI intent visible: what the system is doing and why
  • Encourage automation without eroding trust: transparency + control
  • Scale patterns: reusable interaction models across chat, voice, and device UIs

Outcomes (high level)

Detailed metrics and artifacts are confidential, but these initiatives contributed to clearer AI-led resolution paths, stronger trust signals in automated support, and scalable patterns for conversational and device-based customer service experiences.

Confidentiality note: Visual designs, detailed flows, and specs for these projects are not public. This page describes the scope, approach, and design themes without sharing confidential artifacts.
