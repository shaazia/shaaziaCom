The Problem

Customers often reach support at moments of confusion, urgency, or frustration—subscription changes, unknown charges, device issues, and delivery disruptions. These are high cognitive-load moments that can quickly escalate to human support when clarity or confidence is missing.

My Role

Lead Designer

Conversational & Proactive Support Experiences

  • Designed AI-driven support across chat, voice, and multimodal surfaces
  • Partnered with product, engineering, applied science, and operations
  • Focused on high-volume, high-impact issues where confusion drives contact

What I Designed

Key experiences designed across Prime Video, Amazon Music, Kids+, and Kindle Unlimited.

Subscription Management Conversations

Chatbot experiences enabling customers to cancel, update, or modify subscriptions with clear consequences, transparent language, and reduced emotional friction.

Billing & Unknown Charge Resolution

Conversational flows that guide customers through unfamiliar charges—context, explanation, and next steps— designed to reduce anxiety and escalation.

Proactive, Multimodal Self-Service

Next-gen interfaces that proactively detect common issues (connectivity, outdated software, cache/performance) and offer one-click resolution—before customers contact support.

Service Continuity & Payment Readiness

Proactive alerts for expiring cards or payment issues to prevent interrupted service and failed purchases.

Cross-Device Conversational Continuity

Designed conversations that persist across Echo devices (Alexa+), mobile, and desktop—preserving intent, context, and progress.

Proactive Delivery Notifications

Early notifications for weather-related delays and delivery risks to set expectations and reduce uncertainty-driven contact.

Impact & Outcomes

Reduced Customer Effort

Designed clear, action-forward flows that help customers resolve issues faster with less confusion.

Increased Self-Service Success

Improved completion paths across AI-driven channels—minimizing unnecessary escalation.

Prevented Avoidable Contacts

Proactive detection and one-click fixes helped customers resolve issues before reaching support.

Built Trust Across Devices

Cross-device continuity ensured customers didn’t lose context, progress, or confidence.

Why This Work Matters

This program shows how conversational AI and proactive design can create support experiences that are calmer, clearer, and more scalable—while still feeling human.
