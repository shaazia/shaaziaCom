Amazon Customer Service

A collection of confidential initiatives designing AI-driven customer support across chat, voice, and devices— focused on reducing customer effort, increasing automation, and scaling trusted interaction patterns.

AI Chatbots Voice UX Multimodal and touch Future Vision Devices & Entertainment Retail

Context

This page represents multiple Amazon Customer Service programs that are not yet public. The work spans conversational AI design, proactive support experiences, and cross-device continuity across chat, voice, and device surfaces. The shared goal: help customers resolve issues quickly and confidently—often before they need to contact support.

The design goal: make automated support feel clear, trustworthy, and action-oriented—especially in high-stakes moments like billing confusion, subscription changes, and device troubleshooting.

Overview (STAR)

Situation

Customers often reach support during moments of confusion, urgency, or frustration—managing subscriptions, investigating unfamiliar charges, resolving device issues, or navigating delivery disruptions. These are high cognitive-load scenarios where unclear paths can lead to escalation.

High-effort moments drive unnecessary contact when reassurance and next steps aren’t clear.

Task

Design AI-driven customer support experiences that reduce customer effort, improve successful self-service, and maintain trust—across chat, voice, multimodal surfaces, and notifications—while supporting cross-device continuity.

Action

I led interaction and conversation design across multiple high-volume customer service programs, focusing on scalable patterns, clear state design, and action-forward guidance. Work included subscription management, billing/unknown charge resolution, proactive troubleshooting, service continuity, and proactive notifications across Amazon experiences.

Result

These initiatives contributed to a shift from reactive support toward proactive resolution—helping customers resolve issues faster, reducing avoidable escalation, and creating reusable interaction models across chat, voice, and device UI surfaces.

My role

  • Staff-level IC design across chat, voice, multimodal, and device surfaces
  • Interaction design, conversation design, and content strategy
  • State modeling, error recovery, and confidence-building patterns
  • Prototyping + future-vision storytelling to align stakeholders in ambiguous spaces
  • Partnership with product, engineering, research, and applied science

Project areas

1) AI customer service chatbots

Designed AI-driven chatbot experiences for high-frequency customer needs including subscription management and unknown charges. The focus was reducing friction, building trust, and translating complex account signals into clear “next best actions.”

  • Subscription management: cancel, update, or modify subscriptions across Prime Video, Amazon Music, Kids+, and Kindle Unlimited
  • Unknown charges: guided investigation flows, clear explanations, reassurance, and escalation options
  • Key UX themes: explainability, error recovery, confidence-building language, reduced handoffs
Transparency reduces anxiety—and anxiety drives contact.

2) Proactive, multimodal self-service

Designed next-generation customer service interfaces that proactively surface and resolve common issues before customers contact support. These experiences helped customers quickly identify causes (connectivity, software updates, cache/performance) and resolve them with one click.

  • Proactive detection and guided troubleshooting to reduce “search and guess” behavior
  • Action-forward UI that makes diagnosis and resolution feel predictable
  • Designed to prevent avoidable contact and reduce time-to-resolution
The best support interaction is the one customers never need to start.

3) Service continuity & proactive notifications

Designed proactive experiences that protect uninterrupted service and reduce uncertainty-driven contact—such as alerts for expiring payment methods, delivery disruptions (including weather-related delays), and account issues that customers can resolve immediately.

  • Payment readiness: proactive alerts for expiring credit cards to avoid interruptions
  • Delivery notifications: expectation-setting messaging and clear next steps
  • Design focus: early signal, calm tone, immediate action paths

4) Cross-device conversational continuity (Echo / Alexa+ and beyond)

Designed experiences that allow customers to seamlessly continue support conversations across devices—such as Echo family products (Alexa+), the Amazon app, and desktop—preserving context, intent, and progress so customers don’t have to repeat themselves.

  • Conversation state design to support handoffs and resumable flows
  • Repair strategies for ambiguity and partial input in voice contexts
  • Multimodal handoffs when a task benefits from visuals or deeper account detail

Design principles across the work

  • Reduce cognitive load: translate system complexity into human language
  • Design for ambiguity: clear recovery paths and safe defaults
  • Make AI intent visible: what the system is doing and why
  • Encourage automation without eroding trust: transparency + control
  • Scale patterns: reusable interaction models across chat, voice, and device UIs

Outcomes (high level)

Detailed metrics and artifacts are confidential, but these initiatives contributed to clearer AI-led resolution paths, stronger trust signals in automated support, and scalable patterns for conversational and device-based customer service experiences.

  • Reduced customer effort: clearer paths to resolution during high-stress support moments
  • Increased self-service success: improved completion paths across AI-driven channels
  • Prevented avoidable contacts: proactive detection + one-click fixes for common device issues
  • Improved continuity: fewer repeated steps when customers switch devices or channels
  • Reusable patterns: consistent interaction models across chat, voice, and device surfaces
Proactive design scales better than reactive support.
