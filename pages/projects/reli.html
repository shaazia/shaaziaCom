ReLi

UI and marketing design for ReLi platform.

The Challenge

ReLi, a rent-to-own payment solution, needed to build customer awareness and drive adoption in a competitive market. The platform solved a real customer problem but lacked clear communication of its value proposition and user-friendly experience design.

Our Approach

We designed a modern, accessible platform UI that made the rent-to-own concept clear and compelling. Our marketing design emphasized the customer benefits and simplified the onboarding process. Coordinated design across digital and direct mail channels created a cohesive brand experience that drove adoption and customer confidence.
