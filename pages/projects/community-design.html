Community & Conversation Design

Wireframes and UI for large-scale community platforms.

The Challenge

Large enterprises struggled to build engaged online communities where customers and employees could share knowledge, solve problems, and connect. Existing platforms were rigid, hard to moderate, and failed to foster meaningful interactions at scale.

Our Approach

We designed flexible community platforms that prioritize user engagement through clear information architecture, intuitive moderation tools, and incentive structures that reward valuable contributions. Our wireframes and UI systems support millions of asynchronous conversations while maintaining community health and driving business outcomes.
