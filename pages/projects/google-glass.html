Google Glass

Persona and UI design for wearable technology.

The Challenge

Google Glass represented a new form factor with no established user expectations or best practices. Designing for wearables required rethinking interaction patterns, information presentation, and privacy considerations for a device worn on the face.

Our Approach

We conducted extensive research to understand how people would use wearables in real-world contexts. Our persona development and UI design prioritized glanceable information, minimal cognitive load, and privacy-first interactions. The resulting design patterns became foundational for early wearable experiences.
