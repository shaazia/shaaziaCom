UX Strategy

Strategy and workflow design for digital banking.

The Challenge

A major financial institution needed to transform their digital banking strategy to meet changing customer expectations. Disparate systems, unclear value propositions across channels, and inconsistent user experiences were hurting customer satisfaction and competitive positioning.

Our Approach

We developed a comprehensive UX strategy that defined customer journeys, identified key moments of truth, and established design principles for omnichannel consistency. Our work included user research, competitive analysis, and workflow redesign that guided the organization's digital transformation and resulted in improved customer satisfaction and loyalty.
