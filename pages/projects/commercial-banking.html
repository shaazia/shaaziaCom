Case Study

Commercial Banking

Creating the brand identity, logo redesign, and website experience from scratch for a new commercial banking division at Woodforest National Bank—establishing credibility, clarity, and a scalable design foundation from day one.

Branding UI Design Information Architecture Personas Content Structure

Context

Woodforest National Bank was launching a new commercial banking division and needed more than a website—it needed a distinct identity. The work included a logo and brand refresh alongside a new digital experience that could stand on its own while still feeling connected to the parent organization.

This was a from-scratch initiative. I led the effort as VP of Marketing and Design, serving as lead designer and setting the strategic and visual direction for both the brand and the website experience.

Brand & logo direction

The logo and visual identity were redesigned to signal trust, stability, and modern capability—qualities essential for commercial banking customers making high-value decisions.

The brand system balanced approachability with rigor, ensuring it could scale across marketing, product, and future service touchpoints without feeling dated or overly corporate.

The goal: create a confident, modern brand presence—and make complex offerings feel navigable and approachable.

Approach

As VP of Marketing and Design, I led the end-to-end effort—defining experience principles, information architecture, and visual direction while also working hands-on as lead designer across key flows and templates.

Persona development helped clarify business customer needs and decision styles, informing content structure, navigation, and tone. This ensured the experience could speak clearly to multiple roles while maintaining a cohesive voice.

Design highlights

The visual system was designed to feel trustworthy and modern without becoming “techy.” The UI emphasizes structured layouts, clear hierarchy, and predictable navigation—so users can scan quickly and move with confidence.

Outcome

The result was a cohesive brand and website foundation for the new division—clear structure, consistent design, and an experience built to scale as offerings and content evolved. The work established a strong first impression and a system the team could grow without reinventing the basics.
