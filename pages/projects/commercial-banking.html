Case Study

Commercial Banking

A redesigned dashboard and persona-driven workflows that help commercial banking customers manage complex accounts, approvals, and transactions with less friction—without compromising security or compliance.

UI Design Personas Workflow Design Enterprise UX
A simplified commercial banking dashboard designed for speed, clarity, and oversight.

The challenge

Commercial banking customers often manage multiple accounts, approvals, transactions, and reporting needs at once. The existing platform made routine actions harder than they needed to be—information was scattered across screens, navigation was inconsistent, and key workflows felt slow for power users.

The result: customers spent time hunting for context instead of acting on it. For teams moving large volumes of money, speed and confidence are the product.

The goal was to reduce “search time” and make the next best action obvious—while staying within security and compliance constraints.

Approach

We reframed the dashboard around how commercial users actually operate: quick situational awareness, fast access to high-frequency tasks, and trustworthy signals for risk and exceptions.

Personas anchored the work—capturing needs for different roles (operators, approvers, and decision-makers) and clarifying what “success” looks like for each. From there, we streamlined core flows and reorganized information architecture so customers could move from overview to action without losing context.

Design highlights

The new experience prioritizes clarity and operational flow: a focused dashboard, predictable navigation, and surfaced exceptions that reduce the need to dig through multiple screens.

Outcome

This redesign created a stronger foundation for commercial banking workflows—making the system easier to learn, faster to use for repeat tasks, and more confidence-inspiring for high-stakes actions. The interface emphasizes what matters most: visibility, control, and momentum.
