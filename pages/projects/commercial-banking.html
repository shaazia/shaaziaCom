Commercial Banking

UI and persona design for commercial banking solutions.

The Challenge

Commercial banking customers require complex tools to manage multiple accounts, transactions, and compliance requirements. The existing platform was difficult to navigate, with scattered information and workflows that slowed down critical business operations.

Our Approach

We redesigned the commercial banking dashboard to prioritize business intelligence and operational efficiency. Through comprehensive user research and persona development, we created workflows that reduce friction for power users while maintaining security and regulatory compliance. The new interface enables faster decision-making and improved financial oversight.
