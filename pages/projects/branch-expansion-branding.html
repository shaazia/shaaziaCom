Case Study

Branch Expansion & Branding

I pioneered UX at Woodforest National Bank, leading the creation of a scalable brand and in-branch experience system deployed across 750+ branches in 17 states as Walmart’s largest banking partner.

Brand Systems Marketing Design Guidelines In-Branch Campaign Creative

Context

As Woodforest National Bank expanded into new markets, teams needed a cohesive way to show up consistently— across in-branch, point of sale, and marketing touchpoints—while still allowing for local nuance.

The design goal: create a brand toolkit that scales—so every new branch launch feels familiar, trustworthy, and easy to recognize.

The challenge

Branch expansion creates a classic consistency problem: multiple channels, multiple teams, and high visibility in physical environments. Without clear guidelines, branches risked uneven messaging, fragmented visuals, and slow execution during launches.

Approach

I developed a practical brand system and a set of reusable marketing assets designed for real-world rollout. The work emphasized clarity, repeatability, and flexibility—ensuring teams could deploy materials quickly while maintaining a cohesive identity.

Brand identity foundations

Established core visual building blocks—color, typography, and usage rules—so designers and marketers could confidently produce consistent work across print and digital.

Campaign creative

Designed campaign assets that communicate value quickly, using a clear hierarchy and recognizable brand cues. The emphasis was on “at-a-glance” comprehension—especially in high-traffic, physical retail environments.

In-branch and point-of-sale

Created in-branch signage and promotional materials designed for physical constraints—distance readability, concise messaging, and consistent placement of identity elements.

Visual style guidance

Documented photographic and layout guidance to keep creative cohesive across teams and agencies—helping the brand feel consistent, human, and recognizable.

Launch-ready assets

Built a set of reusable templates and assets that made branch launches easier to execute—helping teams move fast without reinventing the visual language each time.

Outcome

This work established a scalable brand foundation for branch expansion—creating guidelines and assets that improved consistency, reduced rework, and supported faster rollout across channels.
