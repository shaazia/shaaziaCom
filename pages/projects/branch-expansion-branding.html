Branch Expansion & Branding

Branding and marketing for branch expansion initiatives.

The Challenge

Woodforest National Bank was expanding into new markets and needed a cohesive branding strategy to establish local presence and build customer trust. Without clear brand guidelines and marketing consistency, new branches struggled to communicate their value proposition effectively.

Our Approach

We developed comprehensive brand guidelines and a suite of marketing materials for branch expansion. Our strategy included in-branch signage, promotional campaigns, and digital assets that reinforced the Woodforest brand identity while allowing local customization. This approach increased brand recognition and accelerated customer acquisition in new markets.
