Online Account Opening

Analytics and research for online account opening flows.

The Challenge

A financial institution was experiencing high abandonment rates in their online account opening process. Customers were dropping out at critical steps, leading to lost revenue and incomplete customer onboarding. Root causes were unclear due to limited analytics and user insight.

Our Approach

We conducted comprehensive analytics analysis and evaluative research to identify friction points in the account opening flow. Our findings revealed that unclear instructions, excessive validation errors, and unclear error messaging were the primary causes of abandonment. We redesigned the flow based on these insights, resulting in significantly improved completion rates and faster customer onboarding.
