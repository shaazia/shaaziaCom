Infiniti

Wireframes and persona design for automotive UI.

The Challenge

Infiniti needed to design an in-vehicle user interface that could handle complex functions while keeping drivers safe and focused on the road. The interface had to accommodate multiple user personas with different technical sophistication and use cases.

Our Approach

We developed wireframes and personas that reflected Infiniti's luxury positioning while prioritizing driver safety. Our design separated critical controls from secondary features, supported voice interaction, and provided contextual information without causing cognitive overload. The result was an intuitive in-car experience that enhanced the driving experience.
