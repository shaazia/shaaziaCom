What I work on

I focus on designing product experiences where clarity, trust, and usability are critical—especially in moments when users are under time pressure, uncertainty, or cognitive load. Much of my work lives at the intersection of complex systems and human decision-making.

Across my career, I’ve worked on customer-facing and enterprise products at scale, often operating in ambiguous spaces where requirements are evolving, constraints are real, and the cost of confusion is high.

AI-driven customer support & conversational UX

I design AI-powered support experiences across chat, voice, and multimodal surfaces. This includes helping customers resolve issues like subscriptions, billing, access, and device setup—without unnecessary escalation to human agents.

My focus is on making AI behavior understandable and trustworthy: clearly communicating intent, surfacing the right information at the right time, and giving users confidence in what the system is doing and why.

Enterprise & consumer workflows at scale

I design workflows for high-frequency, high-stakes tasks—things users do often and need to get right. These include financial transactions, account management, content access, and operational tools used by internal teams.

The work emphasizes strong information hierarchy, predictable interaction patterns, and clear commit points so users can move quickly without losing confidence or control.

Devices, TV, and multimodal experiences

I’ve designed support and service experiences across voice-first interfaces, TV platforms, and connected devices—where attention is limited and context matters. This includes Alexa-enabled devices, Fire TV, and integrations with entertainment platforms like Prime Video.

These experiences require careful consideration of modality shifts, interruption cost, and how users move between voice, screen, and mobile surfaces without losing orientation.

Systems, patterns, and future vision

In addition to shipping products, I regularly work on future-state concepts and design systems. This includes defining reusable interaction patterns, exploring how emerging technologies might change user behavior, and helping teams align on a long-term design direction.

I’m especially interested in work that reduces long-term complexity—creating foundations that allow teams to move faster and deliver more consistent experiences over time.
