Start with intent

I align on user intent, mental models, and success criteria before moving into UI. This reduces rework and keeps teams focused on the right problem.

Design for trust

In AI, finance, and support, clarity isn’t enough—people need to trust what’s happening. I design explanations, system status, safe defaults, and strong recovery paths.

Systemize patterns

I build reusable interaction models—so experiences scale across teams, surfaces, and future features.

Reduce cognitive load with structure

I use hierarchy, chunking, and progressive disclosure so users see what matters now—while details stay available when needed.

Make states and edge cases first-class

Empty states, errors, latency, and recovery are where trust is won or lost—especially in support and AI workflows.

Prototype early to de-risk decisions

I prototype to learn fast—pressure-testing flows, language, and interaction models before investing in build.

Stay hands-on through execution

I partner closely with engineering through build to refine behavior, polish states, and protect experience quality.

What partners can expect

  • Early alignment in ambiguity
  • Fast prototyping to clarify direction
  • Clear artifacts engineers can implement
  • Attention to states, errors, and recovery
  • Patterns that scale across surfaces

Principles

Clarity is a feature. Trust is a requirement. Systems beat one-offs.
