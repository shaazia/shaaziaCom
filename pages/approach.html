Approach

How I design

I’m most effective in ambiguous problem spaces—where goals aren’t fully defined, constraints are real, and teams need help making sense of complexity.

  1. Frame Clarify intent, constraints, and mental models.
  2. Explore Diverge quickly with concepts and prototypes.
  3. Validate Test decisions, edge cases, and trust signals.
  4. Ship Stay close through build, refine states, iterate.

Start with intent and mental models

Before jumping into UI, I focus on understanding user intent, mental models, and decision points. I look for mismatches between how systems are structured and how people expect things to work.

Framing Decision points Journey logic

Design for trust, not just usability

In domains like AI, finance, and customer support, usability alone isn’t enough. Users need to trust the system—through clear explanations, visible status, predictable outcomes, and strong recovery paths.

Trust signals System status Recovery

Reduce cognitive load through structure

Many UX problems are information problems. I focus on hierarchy, chunking, and progressive disclosure—so users see what matters now, with details available when needed.

Hierarchy Progressive disclosure Calm UX

Design systems, not one-off screens

I think in systems and patterns. Rather than designing isolated screens, I define reusable interaction models that scale across flows, surfaces, and teams.

Patterns Scalability Consistency

Stay close through execution

I stay engaged through build and iteration—reviewing edge cases, refining states, and ensuring the final experience matches the intent of the design.
